# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps create a positive environment:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people who are new
- Respecting different opinions, skills and experiences
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Behavior that is not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Insults, trolling, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing other people's private information, such as an address or email, without permission
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and will explain the reason when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests and discussions, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer at **syedkhalander66@gmail.com**. All reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

The maintainer follows these steps, depending on how serious the behavior is:

1. **Correction:** a private message explaining what was wrong and why. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning:** a warning that continued behavior will have consequences, with no interaction with the people involved for a set period.
3. **Temporary ban:** a temporary ban from all interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban:** a permanent ban for a pattern of violations or serious harassment.
